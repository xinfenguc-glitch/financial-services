# China Financial-Policy Timeline

Source tags: **[W]** = Wright, *Grasping Shadows* (2023). **[post]** = after the report; not from a library source, so verify before citing.

## Credit boom (2008–2016)
| Date | Event | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Nov 2008 | "RMB 4 trillion" stimulus, mostly bank-funded and local-government-led. TSF grows +32% (2009), +24% (2010) | W ch02 |
| 2010 | Ban on direct bank–trust cooperation; channel business shifts to other nonbanks | W ch02 |
| 2011–2012 | Credit growth stays at 18–19% despite recovery and inflation; bank assets double in four years | W ch02 |
| 2012–2016 | PPI negative for four consecutive years; informal lending supplies RMB 36.7 trillion (~31% of new credit) | W ch02 |
| Late 2013 | NCDs introduced | W ch02 |
| 17–25 Jun 2013 | Interbank crisis: PBOC withholds liquidity, rates hit 20–30%, stocks fall >10%, PBOC backs down | W ch01 |
| Apr 2014 | Circular No. 127 limits interbank concealment; activity shifts to receivables and asset-management plans | W ch02 |
| 2014 | Property slows; trust defaults on developer loans; shantytown redevelopment program | W ch02 |
| Nov 2014 | PBOC begins rate cuts | W ch02 |
| 2015 | Lending rates fully liberalized; formal deposit-rate ceiling abolished (informal coordination persists) | W ch07 |
| 2015 | Local-government fund budget runs its first deficit; SRBs introduced | W ch05 |
| 2015–2018 | RMB 12.2 trillion local-debt swap-bond program | W ch03 |
| Mid-2015 | Equity bubble bursts; ~RMB 2 trillion CSFC rescue | W ch02 |
| Dec 2015 | Lou Jiwei: the swap cut local borrowing costs from ~10% to 3.5% | W ch02 |
| Apr 2016 | Commodity-futures frenzy; volumes exceed all Chinese equity exchanges | W ch02 |

## Deleveraging campaign (2016–2019)
| Date | Event | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| 9 May 2016 | *People's Daily* "authoritative personage": "A tree cannot grow to the sky" | W ch03 |
| 24 Aug 2016 | PBOC adds 14-day reverse repos at 2.4%; quiet tightening begins | W ch03 |
| Oct 2016 | Politburo: "curb asset bubbles" | W ch03 |
| Dec 2016 | MPA adds off-balance WMPs; Sealand Securities squeeze | W ch03 |
| Feb 2017 | First OMO rate hike (four hikes, 30 bp in total, through Apr 2018); first draft of the asset management rules | W ch03 |
| Feb 2017 | Peak of "shares and other investments" in banks' uses of funds (12.9%) | W ch04 |
| Apr–May 2017 | Politburo on financial security; seven Xinhua commentaries | W ch03 |
| 2017 | "334 checks" (CBRC) | W ch03 |
| Jul 2017 | National Financial Work Conference; deleveraging framed as a long-term campaign; "aggregates" | W ch03 |
| Aug 2017 | NCDs to count as interbank liabilities (effective 2018) | W ch03 |
| Oct–Nov 2017 | Bond selloff; policy-bank funding squeezed | W ch03 |
| Nov 2017 | FSDC established (Liu He) | W ch03 |
| Dec 2017 | Politburo "three major battles"; CBRC liquidity-management rules | W ch03 |
| Jan 2018 | Liquidity Matching Ratio | W ch03 |
| 2018 | CBIRC formed (CBRC + CIRC merger); term limits removed | W ch03, ch07 |
| Early 2018 | Local implicit-debt disclosure and 3–5 year repayment drive | W ch03 |
| Apr 2018 | Final asset management rules | W ch03 |
| 2018 | Bank asset growth hits a record-low 6.8%; output falls 6–7% (Q3); bond defaults jump (2H) | W ch04, ch05 |
| Jul 2018 | TSF revision: ABS and loan write-offs added | W ch06 |
| Aug 2018 | Thousands protest P2P collapses in Beijing; no bailout | W ch03, ch06 |
| Sep 2018 | SOEs told to cut leverage 2pp by end-2020; perpetual-bond surge follows | W ch03, ch06 |
| Late 2018 | Beijing relaxes local implicit-debt pressure as infrastructure growth falls from 19% to 3.8% | W ch06 |
| Dec 2018 | WMPs moved into separate bank subsidiaries | W ch03 |
| 2019 | Baoshang Bank fails (first bank failure in over two decades); Bank of Jinzhou | W ch01, ch04 |
| 2019 | PBOC stress test "severely adverse" GDP: 4.15% | W ch07 |
| Dec 2019 | TSF revision: central government bonds added | W ch06 |

## Aftershocks (2020–Apr 2023)
| Date | Event | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| 2020 | All P2P platforms closed | W ch02 |
| Aug 2020 | "Three red lines" for developers | W ch03 |
| Nov 2020 | Yongcheng Coal (Henan local SOE) default; Henan issuers shut out for over a month | W ch05 |
| 2021 | CBIRC targets a ~RMB 1 trillion cut in trust loans | W ch03 |
| Jun 2021 | Presale revenue peaks at RMB 16.3 trillion | W ch05 |
| Jul 2021 | For-profit tutoring banned; tech crackdown (Didi, Alibaba, Meituan, Tencent); "common prosperity" | W ch07 |
| Late 2021 | Evergrande defaults (>$300bn debt); cash-type WMP rules | W ch01, ch03 |
| 2022 | Shanghai lockdown; land sales −24%; LGFVs buy 40–45% of land; official GDP 3% | W ch01, ch07 |
| Jul 2022 | Home buyers' mortgage-boycott threats | W ch01 |
| Nov–Dec 2022 | Zero-Covid abruptly dismantled | W ch07 |
| 2022 | Births 9.6 million; population declines | W ch07 |
| Mar 2023 | Li Qiang premier; He Lifeng replaces Liu He; reports of a revived Central Financial Work Commission | W ch07 |
