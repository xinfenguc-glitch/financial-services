**Rating: BUY.** Our rating framework is BUY above +15% expected 12-month upside and SELL below –10%. At HK$45.00 the implied market cap is US$25.3bn, just below the IPO valuation of ~US$26bn. That equates to 22.0x FY2026E and 17.5x FY2027E adjusted P/E, or 8.2x FY2027E EV/adjusted EBIT once pro forma cash is excluded.

**Why the stock looks mispriced.** At HK$30.84 the fully diluted market value is US$17.4bn. The market is therefore valuing the operating business — US$42bn of revenue, 1.1bn orders and 291m customers — at an enterprise value of only **US$3.5bn**. That is **0.08x LTM sales, 2.2x LTM EBITDA and 3.0x LTM EBIT**, against peer medians of 1.4x, 14.1x and 24.1x. Even after deducting a US$1.5bn minimum-cash buffer and a US$1.0bn regulatory reserve, the stock trades at an ex-cash P/E of under 6x FY2027E (price less net cash, divided by EPS excluding after-tax interest income).

## 2. Discounted Cash Flow Analysis

### 2.1 Methodology
- **Cash flows:** unlevered FCF = EBIT × (1 – 22% tax) + D&A (PP&E and intangibles) – capex – Δ operating NWC.
  - Share-based compensation is treated as a real cost, so it is **not** added back.
  - IFRS 16 lease payments are treated as operating costs: right-of-use depreciation is not added back, and lease liabilities are not deducted in the bridge.
- **Explicit period:** a 2H26E stub plus FY2027E-FY2031E, discounted to the 30-Sep-2026 valuation date on a mid-period convention.
- **Cash starting point:** the 30-Jun-2026 balance sheet. The stub FCFF includes the seasonal second-half working-capital unwind of US$0.9bn, which offsets the working-capital float embedded in June cash.

**Choosing our 14x target P/E.** We apply 14x FY2027E adjusted EPS:
- It sits at the **e-commerce-platform median** and at the **25th percentile of all peers**.
- It is well below the apparel peers that SHEIN's operating model resembles.

The discount reflects:
- SHEIN's lower margins (3-4% operating vs. 12-20% for Inditex and Fast Retailing).
- Its trade-policy and regulatory exposure.
- WVR governance.

At 14x, the implied ex-cash P/E is only about 8x (after deducting HK$20.2/share of net cash and the after-tax interest income), because roughly 27% of FY2027E adjusted pre-tax profit is interest income.

**Why not PDD's 7x?** PDD trades on a 3x EV/EBITDA partly because its cash is held largely in China and its core domestic business faces intense competition. SHEIN's cash is mostly US-dollar deposits held through a Singapore-headquartered group, and its board has committed to paying out at least 50% of profit.

## 7. Price Target, Rating and Catalysts

**12-month price target HK$45.00 (+45.9%); initiate at BUY.**

The target blends DCF and P/E equally:
- DCF captures the long-duration value of a business with negative working capital, low capital intensity and a large cash balance.
- P/E anchors the target to how Hong Kong investors actually price consumer-internet names.

We do not roll the DCF forward to a 12-month-forward value, a conservative choice. At our target the stock would trade at 17.5x FY2027E adjusted P/E, a discount to the 20.3x peer median.

### Key catalysts
1. **4Q26 peak season (11.11, Black Friday, Christmas) and FY2026 results (around March 2027).** Management is "cautiously optimistic" on 2H26 adjusted net income; we forecast US$651m vs. US$499m in 1H26. The FY2026 results should also bring the **first dividend**, which we estimate at HK$1.02/share (3.3% yield) under the ≥50% payout policy.
2. **Freight normalisation.** 2Q26 fulfilment costs were inflated by the Iran-conflict spike in oil prices and freight rates. Management estimates adjusted net margin would have been "broadly similar to a year ago" (~6%) excluding this. A partial reversal would be a significant earnings tailwind.
3. **Evidence that EU volumes stabilise** after the 1-Jul-2026 duty change, as happened in the US (US decline narrowed from –14.3% in 1Q26 to –6.0% in 2Q26).
4. **Capital return.** A buyback under the general repurchase mandate, or a payout above 50%, would highlight the cash backing.
5. **Index and Stock Connect eligibility.** Potential inclusion in the Hang Seng Composite Index and Southbound Stock Connect, subject to index rules and WVR eligibility criteria, would broaden the investor base.

## 8. Sanity Checks

- **Market-implied expectations.** At HK$30.84 and 11.2% WACC, the market price implies a steady-state EBIT margin of roughly 1.5-2% with no growth. That is below every annual margin SHEIN has reported (2.5-4.3%) and the 2.4% trough in 1H26.
- **Target-implied multiples.** At our target, EV/FY27E adj. EBIT is 8.2x and P/E 17.5x. Both are below the peer medians of 24.1x EV/EBIT and 20.3x NTM P/E.
- **Private-market marks.** The IPO priced at HK$48.56, and the Series D+ round in 2023 was priced at US$15.02 per share (split-adjusted). Our HK$45 target sits below both.
- **Cash floor.** Pro forma unrestricted cash is HK$23.9/share, 78% of the current price (HK$24.7, or 80%, including investments). After our US$2.5bn of haircuts, net cash is still HK$20.2/share.
