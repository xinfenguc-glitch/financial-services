## 2. Discounted cash flow analysis

### 2.1 Methodology

Unlevered free cash flow is EBIT × (1 − 22% tax) + D&A (PP&E and intangibles) − capex − the change in operating NWC. We treat share-based compensation as a real cost and do not add it back. We also treat IFRS 16 lease payments as operating costs: right-of-use depreciation is not added back, and lease liabilities are not deducted in the equity bridge.

The explicit forecast covers a 2H26E stub plus FY2027E-FY2031E, discounted to the 30-Sep-2026 valuation date on a mid-period convention. The cash starting point is the 30-Jun-2026 balance sheet. The stub FCFF includes the usual second-half working-capital unwind of US$0.9bn, which offsets the working-capital float in the June cash balance.

Global apparel retailers (Inditex, H&M, Fast Retailing) are vertically integrated fashion brands, which is how SHEIN describes itself. We apply 14x to FY2027E adjusted EPS. That is the e-commerce-platform median and the 25th percentile of all peers, and well below the apparel retailers whose operating model SHEIN most resembles. The discount reflects SHEIN's lower margins (3-4% operating, against 12-20% for Inditex and Fast Retailing), its trade-policy and regulatory exposure, and its WVR governance. At 14x the implied ex-cash P/E is only about 8x (after deducting HK$20.2/share of net cash and the after-tax interest income), because roughly 27% of FY2027E adjusted pre-tax profit is interest income.

We do not use PDD's 7x. PDD trades at 3x EV/EBITDA partly because its cash is held largely in China and its core domestic business faces intense competition. SHEIN's cash is mostly US-dollar deposits held by a Singapore-headquartered group, and its board has committed to paying out at least 50% of profit.

The bear case loses about 4% from today's price, against gains of 47% in the base case and 99% in the bull case. The downside is limited because net cash covers about HK$20 per share after our buffers.

## 7. Price target, rating and catalysts

We initiate at BUY with a 12-month price target of HK$45.00 (+45.9%).

The target weights DCF and P/E equally. The DCF captures the long-run value of a business with negative working capital, low capital intensity and a large cash balance. The P/E ties the target to how Hong Kong investors price consumer-internet stocks. We do not roll the DCF forward to a 12-month-forward value, which keeps the target conservative. At the target the stock would trade at 17.5x FY2027E adjusted P/E, below the 20.3x peer median.

### Catalysts

1. 4Q26 peak season (11.11, Black Friday, Christmas) and FY2026 results, around March 2027. Management is "cautiously optimistic" on 2H26 adjusted net income; we forecast US$651m against US$499m in 1H26. The FY2026 results should also bring the first dividend, which we estimate at HK$1.02 per share (3.3% yield) under the policy of paying out at least 50%.
2. Freight normalisation. The rise in oil prices and freight rates during the Iran conflict inflated 2Q26 fulfilment costs. Management estimates adjusted net margin would have been "broadly similar to a year ago" (about 6%) without it, so even a partial reversal would lift earnings significantly.
3. Signs that EU volumes are stabilising after the 1-Jul-2026 duty change, as happened in the US, where the decline narrowed from –14.3% in 1Q26 to –6.0% in 2Q26.
4. Capital return. A buyback under the general repurchase mandate, or a payout above 50%, would draw attention to the cash.
5. Index and Stock Connect eligibility. Inclusion in the Hang Seng Composite Index and Southbound Stock Connect, subject to index rules and the WVR eligibility criteria, would widen the investor base.

## 8. Sanity checks

- At HK$30.84 and an 11.2% WACC, the market price implies a steady-state EBIT margin of roughly 1.5-2% with no growth. SHEIN has reported annual margins of 2.5-4.3%, and its 1H26 trough was 2.4%.
- At our target, EV/FY27E adjusted EBIT is 8.2x and P/E is 17.5x. Both are below the peer medians of 24.1x EV/EBIT and 20.3x NTM P/E.
- The IPO priced at HK$48.56, and the 2023 Series D+ round at US$15.02 per share (split-adjusted). Our HK$45 target is below both.
- Pro forma unrestricted cash is HK$23.9/share, 78% of the current price (HK$24.7, or 80%, including investments). After our US$2.5bn of haircuts, net cash is still HK$20.2/share.
